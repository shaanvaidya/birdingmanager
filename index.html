Birding Manager

Use this starter page to explore what the eBird API can do for your birding workflow. It includes a simple prototype for pulling recent observations and hotspots while keeping your API key in your browser only.

1) Monthly + yearly sightings recap

Answer: “What birds have I already seen this month or year in San Francisco County, and which ones am I still missing?”

  • Use your eBird token to load your personal sightings.
  • Compare against the county list to find missing species.
  • Summarize counts for month and year.

2) Hotspot ideas near a location

Answer: “If I enter a location, what hotspots maximize species diversity right now?”

  • Search hotspots within a chosen radius.
  • Pull recent observations to estimate diversity.
  • Highlight top hotspots with last reported dates.
